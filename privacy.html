Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16/09/2026

Who we are

Nick’s Barbers is a friendly local barbershop providing haircuts, beard trims and grooming services for men and children. We aim to provide a welcoming, professional service to every customer.

Information we collect

Our website does not currently provide customer accounts, contact forms, online bookings or online payments. We do not ask you to enter personal information directly into the website.

If you contact Nick's Barber by telephone, you may choose to provide personal information during the call. We use information you provide to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, help manage your visit.

Cookie notice and optional Google services

On your first visit, our homepage shows a cookie notice asking whether you're happy for us to load three optional features that come from Google: the Google Maps embed showing our location, a live Google Places rating (our Google star rating and review count), and Google Fonts (the typefaces used on this site). None of these three load until you choose "Accept". If you choose "Decline", the site shows a static rating figure, a link that opens directions in Google Maps in a new tab, and falls back to plain system fonts instead.

Your choice is remembered in your browser's local storage so we don't ask again on future visits, and it applies across every page of the site.

Google Maps

If you accept the cookie notice, our website loads a Google Maps embed to display the location of Nick's Barber and help visitors find the shop.

When Google Maps loads or you interact with the map, Google may collect information about your device, browser and use of the service. Google may also use cookies or similar technologies.

Google's processing of information is governed by Google's own privacy policy and terms.

Google Places

If you accept the cookie notice, we use Google Places to display our live Google rating and number of reviews. This involves a request from your browser to Google's servers each time the page loads, and the result is cached in your browser's local storage for up to an hour so it doesn't need to be requested again immediately.

If you decline, this request is not made and the site shows a static rating figure instead. This feature does not create customer accounts or otherwise collect personal information about you.

Google Fonts

If you accept the cookie notice, our website loads the "Fraunces" and "Work Sans" typefaces from Google Fonts. Loading a font this way sends your browser's request, including your IP address, to Google's servers.

If you decline, these files are not requested and the site displays using your device's own fonts instead.

Cookies and local storage

Our website does not use first-party advertising or analytics cookies. We use your browser's local storage for two purposes: to remember whether you have accepted or declined the cookie notice, and, only if you accept, to briefly cache the Google rating figures described above. If you later decline, that cached rating is deleted from your browser.

Third-party services such as Google Maps, Google Places and Google Fonts may use cookies or similar technologies of their own when loaded. These technologies are controlled by the relevant third party.

How we use information

Where you provide personal information to Nick's Barber, we use it for the purpose for which you provided it, such as responding to an enquiry, communicating with you or providing our services.

We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or where we are required to keep it for legal, accounting or business purposes.

Your rights

UK data protection law gives individuals certain rights regarding their personal information. Depending on the circumstances, these include the right to request access to personal information we hold about you and the right to ask us to correct inaccurate information.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), if you believe your personal information has not been handled in line with data protection law. You can find out how to do this at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

If you have questions about personal information we hold about you, please contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services or legal requirements. The latest version will be published on this page with the date of the most recent update.
