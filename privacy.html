Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15/09/2026

Who we are

Nick's Barbers is located at 356 High Street, Sutton, London SM1 1QE. You can contact us by phone at +44 20 8643 5088 or +44 7452 739705.

Information we collect

Our website does not currently provide customer accounts, contact forms, online bookings or online payments. We therefore do not ask you to provide personal information directly through this website.

If you contact us by telephone, we may receive personal information that you choose to provide during the call. We use this information only for the purpose for which you provide it, such as responding to your enquiry or helping us manage your visit.

Third-party services

Our website uses third-party services, including Google Maps and Google Places, to provide location information and display information about our business.

These third-party services may collect information about your device, browser and use of their services. They may also use cookies or similar technologies. Their processing of your information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

Google Maps

Google Maps is used to help visitors find our shop and view its location. When you interact with Google Maps, Google may process information about your device and your interaction with the map.

Google Places and reviews

We use Google Places to display information about Nick's Barbers, including our Google rating and review information.

The information displayed through Google Places is provided by Google. We do not use this feature to create customer accounts or collect personal information directly from visitors to our website.

Cookies

Our website does not use first-party advertising or analytics cookies. We use a small browser preference to remember your cookie settings.

Third-party services used on the website may set their own cookies or use similar technologies. You can control cookies through your browser settings and, where provided, our cookie preferences.

How we use personal information

If you provide personal information to us, we use it only for legitimate business purposes, such as responding to enquiries, communicating with customers or providing our services.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or where we are required to keep it for legal or business purposes.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law gives you rights over your personal information. These include the right to request access to information we hold about you and to ask for inaccurate information to be corrected.

If you have a question about how we handle your personal information, please contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services or legal requirements. The latest version will always be published on this page with the date of the most recent update.
