Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15/09/2026

Who we are

Nick's Barbers is located at 356 High Street, Sutton, London SM1 1QE. You can contact us by phone at +44 20 8643 5088 or +44 7452 739705.

Information we collect

Our website does not currently provide customer accounts, contact forms, online bookings or online payments. We do not ask you to enter personal information directly into the website.

If you contact Nick's Barbers by telephone, you may choose to provide personal information during the call. We use information you provide to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, help manage your visit.

Google Maps

Our website uses Google Maps to display the location of Nick's Barbers and help visitors find the shop.

When Google Maps loads or you interact with the map, Google may collect information about your device, browser and use of the service. Google may also use cookies or similar technologies.

Google's processing of information is governed by Google's own privacy policy and terms.

Google Places

We use Google Places to display information about Nick's Barbers, including our Google rating and number of reviews.

The rating and review information is provided by Google. Our website does not use this feature to create customer accounts or collect personal information directly from you.

Cookies and local storage

Our website does not use first-party advertising or analytics cookies. We use your browser's local storage to remember whether you have accepted or declined the cookie notice displayed on our website.

Third-party services such as Google Maps may use cookies or similar technologies when their services are loaded. These technologies are controlled by the relevant third party.

How we use information

Where you provide personal information to Nick's Barbers, we use it for the purpose for which you provided it, such as responding to an enquiry, communicating with you or providing our services.

We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or where we are required to keep it for legal, accounting or business purposes.

Your rights

UK data protection law gives individuals certain rights regarding their personal information. Depending on the circumstances, these include the right to request access to personal information we hold about you and the right to ask us to correct inaccurate information.

If you have questions about personal information we hold about you, please contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services or legal requirements. The latest version will be published on this page with the date of the most recent update.
